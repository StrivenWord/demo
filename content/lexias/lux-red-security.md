The caseworker may use the county's designated AI screening tool to generate a preliminary eligibility or risk assessment during a benefits intake. The tool must appear on the county's approved AI tool list. Client data entered into or generated by the tool must not leave county systems. The caseworker may not transmit client information -- including case summaries, household data, income records, or AI-generated assessments -- to any personal account, personal device, or outside server. All approved tools are auditable by IT.
